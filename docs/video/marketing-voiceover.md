# LiteraSift marketing video: voice-over script

Silent captioned video; this script is the voice-over, scene by scene.

## Scene 1 (0s)

Literature surveillance shouldn't take weeks. Missed citations, scattered evidence, cases rebuilt line by line.

## Scene 2 (5s)

Introducing LiteraSift. From PubMed search to a reviewed, auditable ICSR, in one workspace.

## Scene 3 (10s)

Search a drug and find every case and citation, whether it was entered as the generic or a brand name.

## Scene 4 (16s)

Every import is reconciled against PubMed. Gaps stay visible and resume without losing work.

## Scene 5 (22s)

Patients, drugs and events are extracted automatically, each linked to the exact sentence it came from.

## Scene 6 (29s)

Work flows from screener to QC to medical review, and managers or medical reviewers can re-route it anywhere.

## Scene 7 (35s)

Seriousness, listedness and reporting deadlines at a glance, with indication, action taken, dechallenge and rechallenge, and confounding flagged.

## Scene 8 (44s)

An evidence-linked narrative, then CIOMS I and E2B R3 outputs, validated and released only after human approval.

## Scene 9 (51s)

Every action is recorded in a tamper-evident audit trail.

## Scene 10 (56s)

Built for human-led pharmacovigilance: E2D minimum criteria, reporting clocks, independent QC, E2B validation, and original evidence kept apart from machine suggestions, on your own infrastructure with your licensed MedDRA.

## Scene 11 (63s)

LiteraSift. Screen smarter. Report with confidence. Version zero point zero one, developed by Dr Sriram.
